═ GAME OVERVIEW ═

Released in 1994 for the Super Nintendo Entertainment System, Final Fantasy VI is widely regarded as one of the greatest RPGs ever created. Set in a fantasy world where magic and technology coexist, the game tells an epic tale of rebellion, hope, and the power of human connections.

═ KEY FEATURES ═

  • 14 unique playable characters with deep backstories
  • Revolutionary narrative with an ensemble cast
  • Turn-based combat system with Espers (summons)
  • Rich steampunk-meets-fantasy world design
  • Opera House scene — gaming's most emotional moment
  • Multiple endings based on player choices

═ LORE & TIMELINE ═

The world of Final Fantasy VI has a tragic history…

1000 YRS AGO

The War of the Magi

A devastating war between humans and Espers scarred the world. The conflict ended with the sealing of Espers into another dimension and the banishment of magic.

PRESENT

The Gestahlian Empire Rises

Emperor Gestahl discovers the Esper world and begins harnessing magical energy to create Magitek, a fusion of magic and technology that gives the Empire unparalleled power.

MID-GAME

The Cataclysm

Kefka betrays Gestahl, seizes the Warring Triad's power, and reshapes the world, creating the World of Ruin — a devastated landscape where hope seems lost.

END-GAME

The Returners' Final Stand

The scattered heroes reunite to defeat Kefka at his tower, destroying his god-like power and restoring hope to the world.
